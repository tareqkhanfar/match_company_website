Match ERP Mobile — Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026 · Applies to Android & iOS builds · Bundle ID com.matchprosys.matcherp

In short — Match ERP Mobile is a field‑sales tool your employer installs for its own staff. It talks to two servers: the ERP system your employer runs for its own business, and a small licence server that checks the app is genuinely paid for. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no third party ever sees your data.
  • Sales, purchase and stock records you enter go straight to your employer's own Match ERP server — never to Match Systems.
  • Match Systems only ever sees a device identifier, for licence enforcement.
  • Camera and Bluetooth are used for barcode scanning and receipt printing — nothing more.

01How the app is set up

Match ERP Mobile is business software, not a consumer app. Each company that uses it — a distributor, a wholesaler, a retail chain — runs its own copy of the Match ERP backend, on its own server, under its own domain (for example yourcompany.matchprosys.com). Your employer's system administrator controls that server, decides who on staff can log in, and owns everything stored on it.

Match Systems, the maker of the app, built and maintains the software but does not operate your employer's server and has no standing access to the business data on it. The one thing Match Systems' own infrastructure does see is described in §04.

03Business records

Customer names, phone numbers, prices, stock counts, invoices, payments — everything you'd expect a distribution app to handle — is created by you or your colleagues and stored on your employer's own Match ERP server, the same one their office staff use from a desktop. The mobile app is simply another window onto that one system.

For offline use, a copy of the catalog and any documents you create while offline are cached in an on‑device database. That cache syncs to your employer's server the next time you have a connection and is never sent anywhere else.

04Device & licence data

To stop a paid licence being copied onto unlimited devices, the app sends a small device fingerprint to Match Systems' own licence‑verification server when you first activate it, and periodically afterwards to confirm the licence is still valid:

Exactly four fields: a device identifier, the device model, the OS name and version, and the app's own version number. No name, no phone number, no location, no contacts.

This data is used solely to enforce the licence your employer purchased and is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

05Device permissions

📷

Camera

Scans item barcodes and photographs items you add to the catalog. Photos are attached to that item and uploaded only to your employer's ERP — the app never browses your existing photo library except when you explicitly pick or save an image through the system picker.

🔗

Bluetooth

Connects to a thermal receipt printer in the same room to print vouchers and reports. This is a direct device‑to‑device link — nothing about the print job travels over the internet.

📍

Location (Android only)

Android requires the "precise location" permission before an app may scan for nearby Bluetooth devices — a rule Android itself imposes, unrelated to GPS. Match ERP Mobile never reads, stores, or transmits your coordinates.

🖼️

Photo library

Used only when you choose to attach an existing photo to an item, or save an exported voucher/report image back to your library. The app cannot browse your photos outside that moment.

06What we don't do

✕ No advertising SDKs of any kind ✕ No analytics or tracking libraries ✕ No selling or renting of any data ✕ No cross‑app or cross‑site tracking ✕ No GPS or location logging ✕ No access to contacts, calendar or messages

07Security

  • All traffic to either server — your employer's ERP or the licence server — is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Your login session and API credentials are stored in the device's own secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via the platform's secure‑storage APIs), not in plain app storage.
  • Offline data lives in a local database on the device, protected by the OS's normal app sandboxing.

08Retention & deletion

Business records follow your employer's own retention policy on their Match ERP server — Match Systems has no independent copy to retain or delete. Licence and device data is kept for as long as the licence is active; write to us at the address below and we'll remove it once the licence ends. Uninstalling the app clears everything stored on the device itself.

09Children

Match ERP Mobile is a workplace tool issued by an employer to its staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

10Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page changes with it and the "Effective" date at the top updates. We don't expect that to happen often — the app's data footprint is deliberately small.
